# CC-29 — the public pages that sell: Pricing, For agencies, the Agency trial, Developers, the public report, the three `/check` pages

The v2 versions of the public pages, from the locked artboards (`docs/design/v2/screens/Final-{Pricing,Agencies,AgencyTrial,Developers,Public}.dc.html`)
with every number and sentence from `docs/design/v2/FACTS-proposed.md` as revised in its Part 2, and every
price and limit from `apps/web/lib/billing/prices.ts` and `GATES` (`apps/web/lib/entitlement-v2.ts`).
Where an artboard's figure disagrees with `prices.ts` (Agency $69, $9 a seat), the page says what
`prices.ts` says. Nothing here is visible until a flag is switched on.

## The migration (`20261114000001_v2_029_public_pages`)

- `start_agency_trial_account(p_user uuid, p_name text, p_host text) returns jsonb`: security definer,
  `search_path pg_catalog, pg_temp`, owned by postgres, executable by `service_role` only. It locks the
  user's `users` row; refuses with `{outcome: 'trial_used'}` when the user owns an agency account whose
  trial was used; returns the agency account an earlier call made (this user its owner, `free`, no trial
  used, no subscription, not deleted) as `existing`, or creates one (`kind 'agency'`, Free's limits, the
  owner seat) as `created`. It stores the site unless the account holds one with that host. The site is
  stored without a check: its first audit is the workspace's.
- `accounts.agency_checkout_url`, `accounts.agency_checkout_at` and `accounts.agency_checkout_interval`
  (`month` or `year`): the open Checkout of an agency account, reused only for a submit on the same
  interval; `accounts.agency_trial_consent_at`: when the owner last ticked the trial's consent. These four
  are hidden from clients by the table's per-column grants
  (`pnpm --filter @crawlmouse/web exec vitest run __tests__/v2-029-public-pages.test.ts`).
- `contact_requests (id, email, sites, note, created_at, sent_at)`: RLS on, all revoked from anon and
  authenticated. `contact_requests` holds an address and no user id; its retention is set by CC-28 with
  the template.
- `public_reports_check_uq`: unique on `public_reports (check_id) where check_id is not null`.
- Nothing in `auth` is touched and no cascade is added.

## Known gaps

- **No member can retract a v2 report.** The hide route keys on `audit_id`, and visibility needs a verified
  domain and a claim; takedown is the only way to remove one. This blocks switching `V2_CHECKS_PUBLIC` on
  until the owner controls of the v2 report page exist.
- **Where a trialist lands.** After Checkout, a trialist lands on their personal account until CC-31's
  account switcher exists. A cancel started from `/agency-trial` returns to `/settings/plan`, which needs
  `V2_CHECKS_PUBLIC`.
- **One agency trial per sign-in.** The rule is per sign-in, not per person: agency trials do not use
  `trial_ledger`.
- **Agency Plus requests are stored, not sent.** They are recorded until the mailer (CC-28) or an owner
  routine reads `contact_requests`.
- **The every-slug sweep** needs production's rows in a branch, which only the owner can provide.
