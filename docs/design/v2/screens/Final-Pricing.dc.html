Questions people ask before paying

Do I need a card for Pro?
No. The 14 days start with your email. Agency needs a card because it sends email from your domain.
Which emails will I get?
Three kinds and no others: a sign-in link, a note when a check finds a change, and one reminder two days before the 14 days end.
Can I cancel?
Any day, from Settings, with no call. The account goes back to Free; nothing is deleted.
