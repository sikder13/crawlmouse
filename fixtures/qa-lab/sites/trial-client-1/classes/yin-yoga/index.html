Yin yoga: what we recommend, and why.

We have written this guide to yin yoga for people who would rather do it once and do it well. A common mistake with yin yoga is to treat balance and rest as the same problem. The cost of getting yin yoga wrong is usually time rather than money, which is good news. Most questions we hear about yin yoga start with flexibility, so that is where this page begins.

We revisit our advice on yin yoga every spring, when flexibility is easiest to judge. Good results with yin yoga tend to follow from steady flexibility, not from a single big change. If yin yoga feels overwhelming, pick the step about flexibility and ignore the rest for now. Think of yin yoga as a habit rather than a project, and breathing becomes much easier to manage.
